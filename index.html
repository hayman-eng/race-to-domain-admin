How to play & what the techniques mean

You start with a phished foothold on one workstation. Your goal: reach DC-01 and run DCSync to own the domain. Every action adds to the SOC's detection meter; hit 100% and you are caught. At 50% the SOC starts hunting (all noise goes up); at 80% it contains a host and resets your foothold there.

The trade-off is stealth vs speed. Two ways to get credentials: Credential Dump (T1003) is loud but works on any host you have admin on; Kerberoasting (T1558.003) is quiet because the crack happens offline, but only works on hosts running a service account. Pick your route.

Techniques: Enumerate (T1087/T1046) reveals a host's weaknesses. Exploit (T1190) takes a vulnerable host. Privilege Escalation (T1068) turns a foothold into admin. Pass-the-Hash (T1550.002) moves laterally using stolen creds. Clear Logs (T1070) buys down detection. DCSync (T1003.006) is the win.

Everything is a fictional network and a game model, not a real toolchain. The lesson is the shape of an attack path and why each step is noisy or quiet.
